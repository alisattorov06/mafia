What is Mafia?

A social deduction game of lies and loyalty

Mafia is a classic party game about hidden identities. Most players are honest townsfolk; a few are secret Mafia members who meet by night and strike in the dark. By day, everyone argues, accuses and votes to exile one suspect. The trick is persuading the town you are innocent — while the real killer does the same. Play the full game free in your browser, no downloads, no sign-up.

How to play

Start a mafia game online in under a minute

  1. 01 Open a room — type a name and click “Open a room”. A 6-letter room code appears.
  2. 02 Invite friends — share the code with 5–19 players. Anyone can join from any device.
  3. 03 Night falls — the Mafia silently choose a victim. The Detective investigates, the Doctor protects, and everyone wakes to a new dawn.
  4. 04 Argue by day — discuss, accuse and vote. Convince the town to exile a Mafia member before they outnumber you.
